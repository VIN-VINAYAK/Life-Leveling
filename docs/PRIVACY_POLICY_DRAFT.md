## Information the app handles

Life Leveling account data includes username, email address, password hash, language preference, and game progress. Depending on features used, the service also stores tasks, habits, nutrition and meal logs, fitness profile and workout logs, expenses, notifications, and account activity. Do not enter information you do not want associated with your account.

## How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, provide and save the app's features, calculate progress, secure the service, and respond to support requests.
